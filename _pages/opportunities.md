People interested in joining our group are encouraged to message [Ignacio](/people/)! We can discuss and work together in applying to available fellowships for postdocs (*e.g.*, [Marie Skłodowska-Curie](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)), doctoral programs (*e.g.*, [Paris Science et Lettres graduate program](https://psl.eu/en/research/life-sciences-ds)) or master students. Different backgrounds are welcome: biologists, computer scientists, statisticians, mathematicians, among others.
